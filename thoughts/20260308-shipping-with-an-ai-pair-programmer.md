# Shipping with an AI Pair Programmer Who Never Sleeps

I used an AI assistant heavily while rebuilding `friendly-rotary-phone`.

And after enough cycles, the pattern is clearer:

- AI is excellent at **acceleration**
- AI is mediocre at **prioritization**
- AI is dangerous when you outsource **direction**

When I gave it clear boundaries and concrete goals, velocity spiked.

When I handed it strategic ambiguity, entropy spiked.

---

The practical operating model that worked for me:

- Human defines vector (why, scope, tradeoffs)
- AI executes chunks (scaffolding, rewrites, repetitive glue)
- Human reviews integration points and architectural coherence
- Tests arbitrate disagreements

In this project, that model let me:

- pivot stacks quickly
- preserve conceptual architecture
- stand up tests/notebooks/docs faster than solo pace

---

My current take:

I don’t want AI to replace thought.

I want AI to remove waiting.

That’s a much healthier contract.
